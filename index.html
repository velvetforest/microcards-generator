Sync devices

Sync your library directly between two devices. Open this page on both and keep it open. Your data goes straight from device to device, encrypted. A public relay (ntfy.sh) is used only for the first handshake and never sees your data.
On the other device open this page, tap Sync devices, then enter this code or scan the QR.
